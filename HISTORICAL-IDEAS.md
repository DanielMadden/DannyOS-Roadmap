# Historical DannyOS Ideas

This document preserves product concepts found in earlier Life OS, life-management, and personal-system notes. Inclusion does not indicate current approval, priority, or implementation readiness. Each concept requires renewed product definition, privacy review, and prioritization before entering the active roadmap.

## Daily Check-in

- Record short nightly status updates.
- Track basic environment conditions, including whether key surfaces and floors are maintained.
- Track basic hygiene, including teeth and shower status.
- Capture whether the day included a meaningful win.
- Capture how the user contributed during the day.

## Work and Planning System

- Organize projects, areas, resources, archives, objectives, milestones, tasks, subitems, and sprints.
- Support Kanban views, workflow statuses, sprint planning, sprint reviews, retrospectives, and daily check-ins.
- Maintain a true backlog separate from the active board.
- Maintain an inbox for unorganized work that is processed during each sprint.
- Estimate work using complexity, effort, and uncertainty.
- Plan across daily, weekly, two-week, monthly, quarterly, six-month, and annual horizons.
- Provide calendar integration, scheduling, dashboards, and mobile widgets.
- Present current focus areas and sources of inspiration in a Life Board.

## Creative Work System

- Maintain a Words database for poems, spoken word, rap, ballads, songs, and other written work.
- Track creation and update timestamps, tags, content type, and a stable identifier.
- Maintain a production database for recordings, videos, edits, and future media projects.

## Reflection and Journaling

- Maintain a gratitude journal.
- Maintain a pride journal for accomplishments and positive self-reflection.
- Support reframing regret or shame into constructive learning.

## Health and Fitness System

- Track nutrition, calories, protein, macros, ingredient information, and other intake data.
- Extract Nutrition Facts and ingredients from a photograph.
- Save frequently used foods and support quantity adjustments.
- Track personal fitness records and daily exercise logs.
- Generate workout and rest-day schedules.
- Present fitness statistics and progress trends.

## Decision Support

- Reduce routine decision load by recommending practical choices.
- Preserve user control for consequential decisions.
- Explain the inputs and reasoning behind each recommendation.

### Clothing selection

- Recommend clothing based on weather conditions.
- Account for the day’s calendar and the need for multiple outfits.
- Randomize between equally suitable options when appropriate.

## Vision and Direction

- Maintain a structured vision board for environments, creative goals, careers, communities, travel, lifestyle, and financial independence.
- Connect long-term visions to objectives, projects, and current work.
- Track progress without treating every aspiration as an active commitment.

## Memory System

- Maintain a searchable, timestamped memory layer across approved notes, chats, projects, and events.
- Tag memories and connect related information.
- Apply time-aware relevance or decay so recent information can be weighted appropriately.
- Use AI to summarize information and surface relevant prior context.
- Require explicit source permissions, retention rules, deletion controls, and visibility boundaries.

## Calendar and Event Preparation

- Detect future commitments from approved sources and propose calendar updates.
- Remind users about unresolved events today, tomorrow, and later.
- Break events into preparation steps such as purchasing tickets, packing, booking travel, or reserving lodging.
- Store relevant confirmation links and preparation status.
- Calculate departure reminders using travel time, traffic, and a configurable buffer.
- Prepare navigation destinations for supported vehicle or mapping integrations.

## Messaging Assistance

- Summarize approved message threads.
- Surface messages that may require action or follow-up.
- Draft responses from user-provided intent.
- Require approval before sending messages or making commitments.

## AI Orchestration

- Ask clarifying questions before turning ambiguous information into projects, tasks, memories, or calendar events.
- Route work between local and hosted models based on privacy, quality, latency, and cost requirements.
- Support web research and approved web actions.
- Explain significant automated decisions and preserve an audit trail.
- Keep consequential external actions approval-gated.

## Ambient Assistant Concepts

Earlier notes considered wake words, continuous speech-to-text, speaker identification, and ambient capture. These concepts carry substantial privacy, consent, security, and legal risks. They should remain exploratory unless a future design can provide visible recording state, participant consent, local-first processing, strict retention limits, and reliable deletion controls.

## ContactOS Extensions

- Track boundaries separately from general compatibility scores.
- Support boundary states such as draft, uncommunicated, communicated, honored, and broken.
- Allow boundary importance to affect trust and relationship recommendations.
- Track activities associated with each interaction to identify which shared experiences produce the strongest outcomes.
- Preserve how and where a contact was first met, along with relevant relationship history.

## Promotion criteria

An idea can move from this historical backlog into the active roadmap after it has:

- A clear user outcome
- Defined scope and exclusions
- Privacy and security boundaries
- Integration requirements
- An approval model for external actions
- A measurable first version
- An explicit priority relative to existing DannyOS modules
